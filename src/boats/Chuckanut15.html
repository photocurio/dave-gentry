"Brilliant"

The Chuckanut 15 is a fun, recreational style, tandem (two person) skin-on-frame kayak with a stable hull design that, while still fast, is optimized for comfortable and relaxed use by paddlers of all abilities.

The C15 has a unique shape and is multi-chined, with a wide, slightly V'd bottom for excellent stability, zero rocker in the  keel for solid tracking and a large cockpit for easy entry and exit. The C15 tandem can also be easily paddled solo!
She's designed for messing about on ponds, lakes and bays and is great as day paddlers or protected water expedition boat.

The Chuckanut 15 is 15 feet long, by 31.5 inches wide {4.6m x 80cm}, and weighs about 39lbs {18kg}.

Maximum recommended capacity is 375lbs {170kg}
